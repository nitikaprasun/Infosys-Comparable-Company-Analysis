# Infosys Limited: Comparable Company Analysis

**Author:** Nitika Prasun

A relative valuation of Infosys Limited against four listed Indian IT services peers: **TCS, HCLTech, Wipro and Tech Mahindra**. The analysis uses FY2025 consolidated Ind AS results and NSE share prices at 28 March 2025.

## Results at a glance

| Company | EV / EBITDA | P / E |
|---|---|---|
| TCS | 18.8x | 26.9x |
| HCLTech | 16.1x | 24.8x |
| Wipro | 13.2x | 20.9x |
| Tech Mahindra | 17.2x | 29.5x |
| **Peer median** | **16.6x** | **25.8x** |
| **Infosys** | **15.6x** | **24.4x** |

- Infosys trades at a discount of about 6% to the peer median on both EV/EBITDA and P/E.
- Applying peer-median multiples gives an Infosys value of about **Rs 1,667 per share**, compared with a market price of **Rs 1,571** (NSE close, 28-Mar-2025). The peer-implied range is about Rs 1,343 to Rs 1,906.
- Infosys has the second-highest EBITDA margin in the group (24.1%, after TCS), a return on equity of 27.9% and no borrowings; its FY2025 profit growth (1.9%) was below the peer median, which explains the modest discount.

## Workbook structure

- **Summary**: peer multiples table, conclusion and charts
- **Comps**: share price, market cap, enterprise value, EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA and P/E for each company, with peer mean, median, high and low
- **Operating Metrics**: revenue and profit growth, EBITDA / EBIT / net margins, return on equity, net cash as % of market cap, free cash flow conversion, capex intensity
- **Implied Valuation**: Infosys value per share at peer low / median / mean / high multiples, with a football-field chart
- **Source Data**: every reported figure (blue cells), its unit and source note; Wipro and Tech Mahindra are converted from Rs million to Rs crore
- **Notes**: method, sources and limitations

## Method

1. **Market capitalisation** = NSE closing price on 28 March 2025 x shares (share capital / face value).
2. **Enterprise value** = market cap + borrowings + lease liabilities + minority interest - cash, bank balances and investments. Lease liabilities are included because EBITDA is after Ind AS 116.
3. **EBITDA** = revenue less operating costs before depreciation, finance cost, other income and exceptional items. Other income is excluded because it is earned on the cash deducted in enterprise value.
4. **Multiples**: EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA and P/E (price / reported basic EPS), on trailing FY2025 results. Peer statistics exclude Infosys.
5. **Implied Infosys value**: peer multiple x Infosys EBITDA (converted to equity value by adding cash and investments and deducting lease liabilities and minority interest, then divided by diluted shares), or peer P/E x Infosys EPS.

## Data sources

- Consolidated financial statements in the FY2024-25 annual reports of Infosys, TCS, Wipro and Tech Mahindra. HCLTech figures are the 31-Mar-2025 comparatives from its annual report for the year ended 31 March 2026.
- Share prices: Yahoo Finance daily NSE closing prices for 28 March 2025, cross-checked to the Wipro annual report (NSE price Rs 262.3) and the TCS annual report (P/E 26.9x).
- The annual reports are not included in this repository. Infosys reports are available at https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/annual-report/annual-reports.html

## Limitations

- Trailing FY2025 multiples only; no consensus forward estimates were used.
- Four peers is a small sample, so the median is sensitive to any one company.
- Share counts ignore treasury shares. Tech Mahindra's FY2025 earnings were still recovering, which inflates its P/E.
- HCLTech figures are comparatives from its FY2026 report and may be presented on a restated basis.
